## Quality Rules

- Prioritize relevant industry sites over general directories.
- Avoid purchased links, bulk directory submissions, private blog networks, and reciprocal-link schemes.
- Use descriptive but natural anchor text; do not force the same exact keyword repeatedly.
- A small number of credible broker-dealer, legal, audit, and compliance links is more valuable than a large number of unrelated links.
